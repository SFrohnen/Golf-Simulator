Before scanning

  1. Charge and switch on your Garmin R10.
  2. Disconnect it from Garmin Golf or other apps. The R10 generally only advertises while not connected elsewhere.
  3. Enable Bluetooth on Android; grant Nearby devices permission when requested.
  4. Pair it when Android prompts you (or pair first in Android Bluetooth settings), then press Scan for R10. The app attempts the registration and radar wake-up automatically.

This is an independent personal project, not affiliated with Garmin. Device data stays on your phone. No internet permission is requested.
